    How each body is driven

    Gait
    Speed from the tracked positions blends idle, walk and run. Stride phase advances with distance covered, so feet don't skate.
    Heading
    Runners face their direction of travel. Defenders keep facing the ball and backpedal or shuffle.
    Stance
    The defending team sinks into a stance when slow. Feet stay planted because hip height is solved from leg angles.
    Ball
    The holder dribbles when the ball is below the waist and holds it at the chest otherwise.
    Events
    A shot triggers crouch, jump and release, and the nearest defender contests. A pass triggers the push and the receiver's reach.
    Video pose
    When a player has a 3D skeleton for the frame, the measured limbs replace the synthesized ones. Limbs the camera didn't see keep the synthesized motion.
    Character
    A rigged Mixamo-style .glb replaces the mannequin. The same driver poses its skeleton, so the motion rules above still apply.
